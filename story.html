Our history · Season by season

Every season made us who we are.

From early rivalries to rebuilding together. This is the story of how 1616 became a place to call home.

  1. Pre-season · Season 0

    Finding our footing

    Mergers and civil war shaped the early server. Trippy Gunslingers (TRiP) emerged as the dominant alliance.

  2. SEASON 1

    Rivalries. And a major achievement.

    Civil war continued, with TRiP at number one and RipX at number two.

    Double Kings and Queens rewards
  3. SEASON 2

    A changing lineup

    TRiP earned Queens rewards. SeaWorld (SWLD) finished in the top ten and became the server’s number two.

    The turning point

    It was a tougher season overall. TRiP departed afterward, setting the stage for a new chapter.

  4. SEASON 3

    A family takes shape

    After TRiP’s departure, the players who remained formed Nova Family: Sapphire (NvSP) and Emerald (NvEM).

    A challenging season of rebuilding
  5. Season 4 · In progress

    Growing stronger, together

    Worst Alliance Ever (WaE) joined us, bringing Nova Family to three alliances. Sapphire (NvSP) welcomed European R4s.

    On track: faction victory + Queens rewards
