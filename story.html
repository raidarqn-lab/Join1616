SEASON 0

PRE-SEASON

The Dawn of 1616

None of us knew what we were getting into.

We thought we had downloaded a game about shooting barrels and killing zombies. Simple enough. Instead, we were dropped onto Server 1616 — a strange new world filled with alliances, rivalries, territory, politics, and people from every corner of the planet.

Somewhere between upgrading our HQs and wondering what half the buttons did, 1616 began to take shape.

1616's Story

We settled into our first alliances and started figuring out how this world actually worked.

Some of those alliance choices turned out to be terrible decisions. Others introduced us to people who, four seasons later, are still fighting beside us.

We learned the rules, broke a few of them by accident, made friends, made enemies, and slowly realized that this was definitely not just a game about shooting barrels.

✿

SEASON 1

The Crimson Plague

The Virus Arrives

Season 1 changed the battlefield.

Crimson Plague introduced virus mechanics, Protein Farms, exclusive hero weapons, and a full-scale fight for control of land across the map. Alliances had to coordinate expansion, defend territory, and learn an entirely new set of mechanics while the server descended into beautifully organized chaos.

And somewhere along the way, 1616 discovered one of its specialties: the sneak-attack virus bomb.

The planning, timing, and orchestration behind those attacks became one of the season's defining memories — and they are still remembered as some of the funniest and most entertaining moments we've had in the game.
