# NOVA SAPPHIRE (NvSP)

## Alliance Rules & Member Expectations

### Our Purpose

Nova Sapphire (NvSP) is a competitive, battle-focused alliance built around active participation, teamwork, and strategic discipline.

**We are a mud-fighting alliance.**

You do not need to be the strongest player to belong here. However, our core members must be among the most active, reliable, and committed players on the server.

If your schedule consistently prevents you from participating in regular season battles, NvSP may not be the right alliance for you.

We understand that real life comes first and that availability can change throughout the season. However, when a member's availability or participation interferes with the alliance's ability to achieve its objectives, leadership may request that the member temporarily or permanently transfer to another alliance.

**Membership in NvSP is a commitment to the team, not simply a place to collect rewards.**

---

### 1. Alliance Duel (VS) Requirements

- Members must earn a minimum of **7.2 million Alliance Duel (VS) points daily**, unless leadership has communicated an alternative strategy.
- NvSP uses coordinated VS strategies that may require members to conserve resources, intentionally reduce scoring during designated weeks, or push aggressively during others.
- Members are expected to follow leadership's announced VS strategy rather than prioritizing individual scores.
- Maintaining our position in Diamond League requires discipline and cooperation from every member.

### 2. Alliance Donations

- Members must contribute a minimum of **5,500 alliance donation points daily**.
- Consistent donations are a basic responsibility of alliance membership and contribute to our collective progress.

### 3. Buster Day & Shield Requirements

- Members must use shields on Buster Day whenever they are not actively participating in combat.
- Members are responsible for ensuring their bases are protected when offline or otherwise unavailable to fight.
- Failure to shield can compromise hive security, expose other members, and negatively affect the entire alliance.
- Additional shield requirements communicated by leadership must be followed.

### 4. Hive Formation & Positioning

- Members must maintain their assigned positions within the hive formation.
- Do not relocate or abandon your position when leadership has instructed members to hold formation.
- Maintaining our formation is essential to controlling territory, protecting our hive, and preventing enemy occupation.
- Unauthorized relocation or failure to follow positioning instructions may result in penalties.

### 5. Respect & Inclusion

- All members must treat one another with respect, regardless of rank, nationality, background, or personal differences.
- Racism, sexism, discrimination, harassment, and personal attacks will not be tolerated.
- Members are expected to contribute to an environment where everyone feels welcome and valued.

### 6. Communication & Leadership

- Respectful disagreement, constructive criticism, and alternative strategic suggestions are welcome.
- Aggressive, insulting, threatening, or abusive language directed toward other members, including R4s and R5s, will not be tolerated.
- Members may question decisions respectfully, but once a battle strategy or alliance decision has been finalized, members are expected to cooperate.
- Leadership is expected to communicate clearly, act fairly, and remain open to constructive feedback.

**Disagreement is acceptable. Disrespect is not.**

### 7. Desert Storm Participation

- Members who register for Desert Storm are expected to attend and participate.
- Failure to attend after registering, without reasonable notice or explanation, may result in exclusion from Desert Storm for the following week or longer.
- Repeated absences may affect future eligibility and participation privileges.
- Members should communicate scheduling conflicts as early as possible.

### 8. Activity Requirements

- **24 hours of inactivity may result in removal from the alliance without prior written notice.**
- Members who anticipate being unavailable should communicate with leadership in advance whenever possible.
- Activity expectations extend beyond simply logging into the game. Meaningful participation in alliance objectives is essential.

### 9. Seasonal Kill Requirements

- Members must achieve a minimum of **500,000 kills per season**.
- Members are encouraged to exceed this minimum through active participation in coordinated battles.
- Kill requirements are intended to encourage meaningful combat participation, not reckless or strategically harmful attacks.

### 10. Scheduled Battles & Season Participation

- Members are expected to participate in scheduled season battles and coordinated alliance operations.
- Members must follow battle plans, positioning instructions, and leadership calls.
- Regular attendance and reliability are essential to NvSP's competitive success.
- Members who consistently cannot participate may be asked to move to another alliance, either temporarily or permanently.

**Strength is valuable, but reliability and participation are what make this alliance successful.**

### 11. Alliance Notifications

- Members are expected to enable mobile notifications for alliance-related announcements.
- Members are responsible for staying informed about battle schedules, strategic instructions, and important alliance communications.
- Missing an announcement does not automatically excuse failure to follow communicated instructions.

### 12. Discord & Voice Communication

- We strongly encourage all members to join our Discord server for important updates, planning, and coordination.
- Members are encouraged to participate in Discord voice chat during battles whenever possible.
- Voice participation can significantly improve coordination and reaction times during active combat.
- We recognize that voice communication is not always practical or possible for every member.

## Accountability & Consequences

Failure to meet alliance expectations or follow established rules may result in disciplinary action.

Depending on the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of the violation, consequences may include:

- Demotion in alliance rank.
- Temporary or permanent removal from train rotations.
- Restrictions on or removal of seasonal rewards, where permitted by game mechanics.
- Restrictions on other alliance benefits and privileges.
- Temporary or extended exclusion from Desert Storm.
- Temporary reassignment to another alliance.
- Removal from NvSP for serious or repeated violations.

Leadership will consider individual circumstances where appropriate. However, repeated violations, deliberate disregard for alliance instructions, or behavior that significantly harms the alliance may warrant immediate action.

**Our goal is accountability and improvement, not punishment for its own sake.**

---

## Recognition & Alliance Culture

Nova Sapphire recognizes and rewards members who consistently contribute to the success of the team.

We value players who:

- Show up when their alliance needs them.
- Support teammates, regardless of individual strength.
- Help others learn, improve, and grow.
- Follow strategies even when doing so requires personal sacrifice.
- Maintain a positive attitude during both victories and defeats.
- Take initiative and go above and beyond for the alliance.

We want NvSP to be competitive without becoming toxic, disciplined without losing its sense of community, and ambitious without forgetting that this is a game we play together.

**We do not expect perfection. We expect effort, accountability, respect, and a willingness to put the team first.**

### Our Standard

**You don't have to be the strongest. You have to show up, work together, and be someone your teammates can count on.**

That's what makes Nova Sapphire.

---

## Fairness, Flexibility & Individual Circumstances

We recognize that real life comes first. Work, family, health, emergencies, and other personal responsibilities may occasionally prevent members from meeting alliance expectations.

Our goal is not to penalize members for having responsibilities outside the game. However, we must balance individual circumstances with our responsibility to the entire alliance.

- **Communication matters.** Members who anticipate missing battles, being inactive, or struggling to meet requirements should notify leadership as early as reasonably possible.
- **Exceptions may be granted.** Leadership may make reasonable accommodations for temporary circumstances, particularly when members communicate proactively.
- **Consistency matters.** Exceptions will be considered based on individual circumstances, previous participation, communication, and the impact on alliance objectives.
- **Fairness does not always mean identical treatment.** Different circumstances may require different responses, but all decisions should be guided by consistent principles.
- **Temporary reassignment is not necessarily punishment.** Members whose availability changes may be asked to move to another alliance until they can resume regular participation.
- **Repeated nonparticipation is different from occasional absence.** Members who consistently fail to meet expectations without communicating should not expect the same flexibility as those making a genuine effort.

Leadership will aim to communicate concerns and provide opportunities for improvement when practical. However, immediate action may be necessary when a member's inactivity, positioning, or conduct threatens alliance objectives.

**We will always try to be understanding of individual circumstances, but fairness also means respecting the time, effort, and sacrifices of members who consistently show up for the team.**

Our responsibility is to support our members while ensuring Nova Sapphire remains a strong, active, and dependable fighting alliance.

Being understanding does not mean guaranteeing someone a permanent position in NvSP regardless of their availability. Members can remain valued members of the broader Nova community even when another alliance is temporarily a better fit.
