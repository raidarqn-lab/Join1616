    Sample visual guide

    Where to find your stats

    Use the numbered sample profile and the instructions beside it.

    What each number means

    1 Total Power

    Tap the area labeled (1) to access your Power Details screen. Your Total Power includes troops in your base, so do not enter this number as Hero Power.

    2 Profession Level

    The area labeled (2) shows your Profession Level. Enter the level shown beside the helmet icon. The maximum profession level is 100.

    3 Kill Count

    The area labeled (3) shows your Kill Count. Enter the full number shown beside the crossed-weapons icon. Do not use M, K, or other abbreviations.

    4 Power Details

    After tapping (1), the screen labeled (4) opens. Enter the separate values shown for Hero Power, Building Power, Technology Power, Drone Power, and Decoration Power.
